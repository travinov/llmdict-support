Privacy policy

This policy explains data handling in the first App Store release of LLM Dict for iPhone, which supports OpenAI, and on this support website.

Developer and data contact: Sergei Travinov. llmdict.support@gmail.com

Data on your device

LLM Dict stores recordings and imported audio, enhanced audio, transcripts, processing results, titles, dates, duration, flags and error messages. Your prompts, instructions and settings are also stored on your device.

The last source Live transcript may also be saved separately so it can be reopened. API keys are stored in the system Keychain. Regular recording and local audio enhancement do not require sending content to the developer.

The developer does not provide a server for storing your recordings. App data may be included in device backups managed by Apple, depending on your system settings.

When data is sent to AI services

In version 1.0, before entering a key or sending data, you must explicitly check the consent box in Settings → Services and Keys for OpenAI. Consent is off by default; existing keys alone do not grant consent. The disclosure identifies the data, recipient, and this privacy policy. The app stores the consent version, date, and recipient address on your device. Changing the OpenAI API address resets consent.

The destination depends on the feature, service and API address you select:

Recording transcription
Audio is sent to OpenAI or your configured compatible endpoint to convert speech into text.
Live
While Live is running, the audio stream and session settings are sent to OpenAI or your configured compatible endpoint. In instruction-capable modes, the selected instruction is also sent.
Text processing
The source text and prompt are sent to OpenAI or your configured compatible endpoint to carry out your instruction.
Connection and model lists
Your key or a token obtained with it authenticates requests to the relevant service. The provider also receives network information needed for the connection, including your IP address.

If you change the OpenAI API address, the operator of that address receives requests, content and authentication data. Its data practices may differ from those of OpenAI. Use an address you trust.

Provider retention

Cloud services process data under the terms of your accounts and selected APIs. Retention periods and processing locations may differ; processing may take place outside your country.

The current app does not disable response storage when processing text through the OpenAI Responses API. OpenAI documents default retention of this response data for at least 30 days, subject to exceptions and account settings. Separate security logs have their own retention rules.

For retention and deletion at your provider, consult its settings and documents: OpenAI API data controls. The app does not control provider-side storage or guarantee immediate deletion of data already sent.

Your choices and deletion

  • Regular local recording is available without consent to cloud processing.
  • Uncheck consent in Settings → Services and Keys to block new requests to that service and cancel ongoing cloud processing. This does not delete data already sent to the provider.
  • You can revoke microphone permission in iOS settings.
  • Deleting a library recording with confirmation removes its audio and text. In version 1.0, the separate saved copy of the last Live text is also cleared when it belongs to that recording. Clear Screen alone does not delete the library recording.
  • You can remove a service key in Settings. This does not cancel your provider subscription or delete provider-held data.
  • Deleting a local recording does not delete exported files, sent emails, backups or provider-held data. Manage those copies in the relevant service.

Local data remains on your device until you or the system remove it. For deletion questions, contact llmdict.support@gmail.com. The developer has no remote access to the recording library on your device.

Support requests

When you send an email, support receives your email address, message and any files you choose to attach. The in-app email action adds the app and iOS versions to a draft; you decide whether to send it.

We use correspondence to respond to, diagnose and resolve your request, and keep it while needed for those purposes. You can request its deletion at the same address. Please do not send API keys, passwords or other people’s personal information unnecessarily.

The support mailbox is hosted by Gmail. The email provider’s handling is described in the Google privacy policy.

Website, analytics and sharing

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. This website has no advertising analytics or upload forms, and does not store preferences in cookies or localStorage. Language selection opens the corresponding page.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security: GitHub Pages data collection information.

When you share or copy content, the apps and recipients you choose handle the data you send under their own policies.

Updates and contact

This page is updated when features or data practices change. The current revision date appears at the top. For privacy questions and support, contact llmdict.support@gmail.com.
